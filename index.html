AI agents for
second-hand commerce.

Ivan is an early-stage Korean company building AI agents that take the slow, manual work out of buying and reselling used goods: finding listings, reading their condition, pricing them across markets, and keeping clean books.

What we build

Marketplace monitoring

Agents watch Korean second-hand marketplaces and surface listings that match a buyer's criteria.

Listing understanding

Structured extraction from listing text and photos: model, specs and visible defects, with uncertain cases flagged for human review.

Cross-market pricing

Expected resale value compared across markets, net of platform fees, shipping and VAT.

Auditable ledger

Every sourcing decision, purchase and sale recorded so an owner can see what was decided and why.

A person stays in the loop: agents research and recommend, and a human approves every purchase.

How we build

We are an AI-native team. Our software is written with Claude Code, and our extraction, photo-triage and research agents are designed to run on the Claude API, with evaluations and human-review checkpoints at each step.
